GHWoR Main Menu Music  (v1.0)
=============================
By WitchDoctoR

The main menu music of Guitar Hero: Warriors of Rock for Guitar Hero World Tour: Definitive Edition: all 14 songs
WoR plays in its menus, shuffled by the game like its own menu music.

  Bodies                      Waidmanns Heil
  Jet City Woman              Holy Wars... The Punishment Due
  Chemical Warfare            I'm Broken
  Bloodlines                  Indians
  Ravenous                    Psychosocial
  Hard to See                 Re-Ignition
  The Outsider                Children of the Grave

Taken from WoR's own menu music streams, brought to the loudness of the other menu music mods (WoR mixes them about
12 dB quieter).

REQUIREMENTS
  - Guitar Hero World Tour: Definitive Edition (with its mod support, DATA\MODS).

INSTALL
  Extract the zip into the folder with GHWT_Definitive.exe (it adds DATA\MODS\(GHWoR) Main Menu Music).

  The game loads ONE menu music mod. If another one is installed (for example the GH5 menu music mod), remove it or
  this one: with two, only one of them plays.

UNINSTALL
  Delete DATA\MODS\(GHWoR) Main Menu Music.

ALSO
  Shipped with the GH5 / Warriors of Rock HUD mod: https://github.com/jgoa156/ghwtde-gh5wor-hud-mod
